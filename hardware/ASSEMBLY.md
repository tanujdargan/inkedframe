# Assembly (v2: printed backboard, mitred mat)

Pictures for every step: `assembly-guide.html` (opens offline in any browser), rendered from `guide2.scad`.

## What you need

- **Printed, black:** board left and right halves, joining strip, Boot plunger (6.6 mm), Reset plunger (5.6 mm),
  USB block. **White:** stand base, leg and hinge pin.
- **Printed, white:** 4 mat strips, 4 bow-tie keys, name plates (across, stacked, blank).
- **Reused from before:** clamp, battery tray, 6 panel clips.
- **Hardware (nylon M3):** 12 x M3x6, 2 x M3x8, 2 x M3x10, 18 nuts, 2 zip ties.

## Backboard

1. **Nuts into the back.** Halves outside face up. Press a nut into each hex pocket: 6 along the seam, 4 for the
   battery tray, 2 for the USB block. Tape over them so they stay put when you turn the board over.
2. **Join the halves.** Inside face up, butt the halves together. Lay the joining strip over the seam and screw it
   down with 6 x M3x6.
3. **Plungers and inside nuts.** Drop the plungers into their guides from the inside, flange up: Boot (the longer
   one) in the hole further from the USB end, Reset in the other. Slide a nut into the side slot of each clamp post
   and of each of the 4 stand-socket bosses. They can't be reached once the frame is closed.
4. **Feather.** Face down, USB end at the bottom. Slide the top (antenna) end under the lip, lower the USB end onto
   the pins. Clamp over the USB end with 2 x M3x8, just snug.
5. **Battery.** Tray upright, open end up: 4 x M3x6 from inside, heads in the tray's pockets. Battery in, wire corner
   on the comb side, push until it clicks. Wire into the comb, plug into the Feather from the right.
6. **USB-C port.** Press the female end into the channel from the top, face at the open front and back end against
   the back wall, cable down into the slot; it clicks in. Pull one zip tie tight round the cable just behind the back
   wall, and thread a second through the slot under the channel and round the plug; trim both. Block onto the board
   with 2 x M3x6. Plug the other end into the Feather; coil spare cable away from the top of the Feather (the antenna).
7. **Display.** Wire the driver board to the Feather and lay it in the clear area at the bottom middle.

## Mat

8. **Strips and keys.** Four strips face down, corners pushed together, a bow-tie key pressed into each corner pocket
   from the back (a drop of glue makes it permanent).
9. **Panel.** Into the pocket, display side down, ribbon cable through the notch at the bottom. Press the 6 clips on.
10. **Name plate.** Turn the mat over and drop a plate into the recess, letters out. The acrylic holds it; lift it out
    with tape. Stacked plate for portrait, blank for no name.

## Frame

11. Acrylic, then the mat with the panel. Connect the ribbon cable to the driver board. Backboard in, inside face
    towards the panel, and close the frame's tabs.
12. **Desk.** Slot the leg's three barrels between the base's two and push the printed pin through all five, head
    first from the left; it's a snug fit in the last barrel. Screw the base onto the landscape socket (middle) or the
    portrait socket (left of centre, seen from the back) with 2 x M3x10 from outside. The leg opens until its tails
    press on the board (about 18 degrees of lean) and folds down flat.

13. **Wall.** Take the stand off. Landscape hangs on the two top holes; portrait (frame turned clockwise) on the two
    down the right edge as seen from the back. The top corner hole works both ways.
